1. Project identity

Project name: SmartStructureBot
Platform: Python + MetaTrader 5
Primary purpose: Fully automated market-structure continuation trading system.

The system is not primarily a generic retracement detector, indicator, or pattern scanner.

Its real objective is:

Identify the main market trend
→ detect a counter-trend move
→ detect when that counter-trend fails
→ enter immediately when the main trend resumes
→ manage the position using structure
→ learn which instruments, sessions, setups, and management rules perform best

The central strategic insight is:

Failed retracement BOS
=
Retracement finished
=
Trend continuation resumed
=
Entry candle

The bot must not wait until the whole retracement has visually completed and then enter late.

4. Market structure hierarchy

The system uses several structural layers.

Typical conceptual hierarchy:

Master / major structure
ATR / tradeable internal structure
Flow / micro structure

These layers must not be treated as interchangeable.

Master structure

Purpose:

Define the dominant market direction and phase.

It should represent meaningful market swings, not every small fluctuation.

ATR / tradeable structure

Purpose:

Locate meaningful tradeable swings,
BOS events,
protected structure,
pullbacks,
and continuation triggers.
Flow / micro structure

Purpose:

Observe lower-level movement,
internal sequencing,
and possible early confirmation.

Flow structure may assist decisions but should not override valid master context by itself.

5. Swing detection rules

The bot should not classify every wick or spike as meaningful structure.

Initial swing candidate

A common candidate model is a validated fractal:

Swing high

A candle high that exceeds the highs on both sides.

For a five-candle fractal:

candidate high > highs of previous 2 candles
candidate high > highs of next 2 candles
Swing low
candidate low < lows of previous 2 candles
candidate low < lows of next 2 candles
Candidate versus locked swing

A fractal is only a temporary turning point.

It becomes structurally meaningful when subsequent price action validates it.

Conceptually:

A potential swing low becomes meaningful when price moves upward
and breaks relevant opposing structure.

A potential swing high becomes meaningful when price moves downward
and breaks relevant opposing structure.
ATR filtering

ATR should help remove insignificant noise.

However:

ATR filtering must reduce noise,
not destroy valid early continuation structure.

The system must preserve sufficiently meaningful mini-swings needed for failed-retracement entries.

6. Structure classification

Highs and lows are classified as:

HH = Higher High
HL = Higher Low
LH = Lower High
LL = Lower Low
Bullish structure

Typical confirmation:

HH + HL sequencing

Strong confirmation may include:

3 HH and 2 HL

A clean market may sometimes be accepted with:

2 HH and 1 HL

provided broader context supports bullish direction.

Bearish structure
LH + LL sequencing

The bot must distinguish between:

main-trend structure
counter-trend structure
temporary compression
noise

A single local HH does not automatically mean the whole market is bullish.

A single local LL does not automatically mean the whole market is bearish.

8. Break of Structure definition

This is one of the most important rules.

BOS requires a candle-body close

A BOS occurs when a candle body closes beyond a meaningful structure level.

Bullish BOS
Candle body closes above the relevant swing high / seller structure.
Bearish BOS
Candle body closes below the relevant swing low / buyer structure.
Wicks do not count
A wick through structure is not a confirmed BOS.
One candle is enough

A valid BOS does not require several confirmation candles.

One candle body close can confirm the break.
BOS must know what it broke

Every BOS event should record:

direction
candle index
break price
broken structure index
broken structure type
broken structure level
body-close confirmation
role
quality
importance
trend alignment

Possible BOS roles include:

TREND_BOS
ENTRY_BOS
FAILED_RETRACEMENT_BOS
CHOCH
EXTENSION_BOS
NEW_SETUP_BOS

These roles must not be mixed together.

9. Core strategy: trend continuation after failed retracement

This is the most important section.

The strategy does not enter merely because a retracement exists.

It enters because the counter-trend move fails.

Bullish-trend setup
1. Higher-timeframe/main context is bullish.
2. Price begins moving downward against the bullish trend.
3. The bearish pullback creates local bearish structure, such as LH and/or LL.
4. The bot identifies the relevant pullback high / mini-protected high.
5. Buyers return.
6. A candle body closes above that relevant pullback high.
7. That break proves the bearish counter-trend failed.
8. The BOS candle is the bullish entry candle.

In compact form:

Bullish trend
→ bearish pullback
→ bearish counter-structure
→ counter-structure fails
→ body close above pullback LH/high
→ BUY on BOS candle close
Bearish-trend setup
1. Higher-timeframe/main context is bearish.
2. Price begins moving upward against the bearish trend.
3. The bullish pullback creates local bullish structure, such as HH and/or HL.
4. The bot identifies the relevant pullback low / mini-protected low.
5. Sellers return.
6. A candle body closes below that relevant pullback low.
7. That break proves the bullish counter-trend failed.
8. The BOS candle is the bearish entry candle.

Compact form:

Bearish trend
→ bullish pullback
→ bullish counter-structure
→ counter-structure fails
→ body close below pullback HL/low
→ SELL on BOS candle close
10. Retracement start

The retracement should not be detected only after a huge move has already occurred.

A counter-move may begin when price first produces credible structure against the dominant trend.

In a bearish main trend

A bullish pullback may be considered active when local bullish intent appears, such as:

price breaks a relevant local bearish structure upward
or
creates meaningful bullish HH/HL behaviour
In a bullish main trend

A bearish pullback may begin when:

price breaks relevant local bullish structure downward
or
creates meaningful bearish LH/LL behaviour

The bot should not require an unnecessarily large number of counter-structure points before acknowledging a pullback.

11. Retracement maturity

Waiting for two or more counter-structure points in every case can create late entries and missed trades.

Therefore:

One meaningful counter-structure point may be enough,
but only when the continuation BOS is strong and convincing.

Recommended logic:

2+ valid counter-structure points
→ normal confirmation requirements

1 valid counter-structure point
→ allowed only with stronger BOS quality and momentum

The system should be:

slightly aggressive,
but not reckless

It should not wait unnecessarily, but it must reject weak first bounces.

12. Retracement end

This must be unambiguous.

The retracement ends on the failed-retracement BOS candle.

It must not end tens of candles after the entry.

Therefore:

counter_move_end_index
=
entry_bos_index

The old retracement engine may still describe the broader pullback for analysis, but it must not control the final execution timing.

13. Entry timing

Entry should happen:

immediately at the close of the failed-retracement BOS candle

Do not wait for:

another confirmation candle
a retest
the full retracement zone to finish
a second BOS
a later trend BOS

unless the strategy explicitly adds such a mode later.

Entry price

The intended entry is based on the BOS candle close.

The broken trigger level is not the entry price.

Example:

Trigger level = structure level broken
Entry price = BOS candle close / executable market price after close

The stored break_price must be checked carefully.

It should not accidentally use:

max(open, close)
min(open, close)

when the intended execution price is the actual candle close.

For live trading, execution will occur at the first available market price after the BOS candle closes, including spread and slippage.

14. Entry direction
Bullish failed-retracement BOS → BUY
Bearish failed-retracement BOS → SELL

The system must never invert direction because a local counter-trend structure was detected.

15. BOS strength and momentum

A structural close alone may be technically valid but commercially weak.

The system should score the continuation candle using:

Suggested classifications:

STRONG_BOS → accept

16. Impulsive versus corrective movement

The bot must distinguish market intent.

Impulsive movement

Characteristics may include:

large bodies
strong directional closes
ATR expansion
little overlap
fast displacement
multiple structures taken quickly
limited opposing wick rejection
Corrective movement

Characteristics may include:

small bodies
heavy overlap
alternating candles
weak closes
slower progress
compression
frequent minor swings

A continuation BOS should preferably be impulsive relative to the corrective pullback it ends.

This relationship matters more than raw candle size alone.

17. Compression awareness

A human may see one pullback where code sees many tiny retracements and continuations.

The system should merge micro-noise into one compression sequence when:

price overlaps heavily
range contracts
mini BOS events lack displacement
swings remain tightly clustered
no meaningful expansion occurs

Compression should not repeatedly reset or generate multiple entries from the same unresolved setup.

18. Protected structure

There are two important concepts:

master/live structure

These must not be confused.

Bullish trend protection

The meaningful protected structure is usually:

Bearish trend protection

Usually:

the last defended LH before a confirmed bearish continuation BOS
Important protection rule

Protected structure should not jump to every newly printed swing.

It should update only when the market proves that the newer swing truly caused or defended meaningful continuation.

19. No future-data leakage

This is a critical requirement.

At every historical candle i, the system may use only information available at or before i.

Forbidden examples:

using later candles to classify an earlier entry
choosing a swing because future price later validated it
selecting the best continuation from the full 400-candle window
using a future retracement end to validate an earlier BOS
using final trend context to judge trades that occurred before that context was known

Every event should ideally contain:

detected_at_index
confirmed_at_index
source_structure_index

Backtests must process candles sequentially.

No full-dataset hindsight should influence historical entries.

This is one of the highest-priority audit items.

20. Active setup ownership

There should be one authoritative owner/director for each active setup.

The system must avoid several engines independently deciding:

Recommended ownership:

Trade Brain owns final action.

There should be only one final entry object.

The code must not approve a normal-risk trade during CHOCH merely because other scores are high, unless explicitly designed.

30. Stop-loss rules
M1 entries

The intended structure rule is:

Stop is based on the relevant swing high/low candle close.

A wick through that level does not automatically stop the trade.

Only a candle BODY CLOSE beyond the invalidation level confirms stop/invalidation.

For BUY:

body close below protected low / setup invalidation structure

For SELL:

body close above protected high / setup invalidation structure
M5 entries

The M5 stop should be more tolerant because M5 contains larger intrabar noise.

Use:

ATR-adjusted protection around the relevant pre-BOS protected swing

A small body close barely beyond a minor level should not automatically invalidate if the configured ATR/body-close logic says otherwise.

Live broker reality

MT5 hard stops are triggered by price, including wicks.

Therefore the architecture may need:

broker emergency stop far enough away
+
software-managed body-close invalidation

This distinction must be explicitly coded and tested.

31. Stop placement structure

The stop should be based on the structure that invalidates the setup.

Not simply:

fixed ATR from entry
nearest random swing
latest low/high regardless of role

The system should store:

invalidation structure index
body invalidation level
emergency broker stop
logical stop condition
32. Trailing rules

Primary preference is structure-based trailing rather than a fixed TP.

Bullish trade

As continuation forms new structure:

trail beneath meaningful HLs
Bearish trade
trail above meaningful LHs

Do not trail to every micro swing.

Only meaningful continuation structure should move protection.

Earlier management ideas included:

break-even near 0.5 ATR
partial around 1 ATR
trail after 2 ATR
lock profit around 1.5 ATR

These were examples, not immutable final rules.

The final preference is:

structure-aware management
with measured ATR assistance

36. Re-entry rule

One re-entry may be allowed per setup.

Scenario:

Initial entry invalidates or software stop triggers.
The original directional setup remains structurally valid.
A new continuation BOS forms in the original direction.
One re-entry is permitted.

If stopped again:

abandon that setup

The bot must track re-entry count per setup, not globally.

39. Filter philosophy

The user explicitly does not want excessive filtering.

The system previously produced too few trades.

Do not add many arbitrary hard filters.

poor setup → reject

Not:

twenty strict filters → almost no trades
