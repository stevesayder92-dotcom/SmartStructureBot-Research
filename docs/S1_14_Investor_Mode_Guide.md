# Investor-mode guide

Investor mode is an honest explanation of the prototype, not a profitability
claim. Start with `S1-CASE-02` for M1 early-entry evidence and use
`S1-CASE-10` as the losing/risk-discipline example.

Recommended sequence:

1. Show the permanent research, orders-disabled and causality badges.
2. Explain HTF direction and the M5 pullback on the parent chart.
3. Jump to the next entry and show the M1 owned trigger and logical stop.
4. Compare engine recommendations with the single Director action.
5. Step through target/protection changes and the trade story.
6. Show one losing example and its risk boundary.
7. Open Debug briefly to prove immutable event hashes and no future rows.

Always state: “Historical research replay. No live orders. Performance is not
guaranteed.” Do not claim that the curated sessions represent expected returns.
The visual mode is investor-presentation ready as a research prototype; the
strategy remains subject to Steve's event-by-event review before demo execution.
