# Bot-brain panel glossary

The brain panel explains what the canonical system knows at the selected
event. It does not calculate strategy in the browser.

- HTF context: H1/M30/M15 causal approval and direction.
- Market structure: dominant trend and current phase.
- Control/transition: authority and recovered/active transition state.
- Impulse: owned directional cycle.
- Retracement/Fibonacci: qualification, remaining impulse and depth.
- M1 quality: child state, grade and rejection/acceptance evidence.
- Continuation: failed-retracement recovery state.
- Target: TP1 lifecycle and price.
- Protection: canonical structure and level.
- Re-entry: lifecycle and attempt count.
- Validation: hard/soft decision evidence.
- Setup: stable identity, status and grade.
- Integrity: causal, schema, ownership and order safety.

Unavailable means the canonical root did not publish legal evidence yet; it is
not permission for the UI to infer a value.
